# 10. Scope and Roadmap

[← Back to contents](../README.md)

## Version one: Chapter One — Light

Version one introduces the world of Light. It starts by introducing Enoch, a young raccoon and repairman, and its goal is for the player to repair the city's power grid and lights.

### In scope

- The prologue in the Hollow.
- Act 1: several repair missions without the serpent, through which Enoch earns recognition.
- Act 2: Enoch is chosen and earns the serpent.
- The city of Light and its districts (see [World](04-world.md)).
- The repair loop: fault tracing, salvage, repair interactions, and power restoration.
- Grid capacity and the choices about what stays lit.
- Context and leveling: Enoch's level and skills, context from experience and from technology through the serpent, and the serpent's abilities.
- Abominations of Light, handled through avoidance.
- The descent through the Underworks to the core (Draft; see [Open Questions](open-questions.md)).
- The first temptation and the illuminate ability (Draft; depends on the item above).

### Out of scope for version one

- The other five gods and their realms.
- Travel beyond Light's lands.
- The Cherub and the dog tribe as active characters (hints only).
- Combat systems.
- The seventh day, the Convergence, and the endings.
- Other playable tribes.

## Milestones (Draft)

| Milestone | Goal | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **0. Pre-production** | Lock the GDD for Chapter One | Open questions for version one resolved; core loop and districts approved |
| **1. Prototype** | Prove the repair loop is fun | One graybox district with fault tracing, salvage, three repair types, and power restoration |
| **2. Vertical slice** | Prove the game's feel | The Hollow and Lamp Row at near-final quality, with leveling, the serpent, barks, and the first machines read through the serpent |
| **3. Alpha** | All content in | Every district playable start to finish, with placeholder art where needed |
| **4. Beta** | Content complete and polished | Final art and audio, tuned pacing, accessibility features |
| **5. Release** | Version one ships | |

## Risks (Draft)

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Repair gameplay becomes repetitive | Introduce a new repair type per district; keep each repair short; make finding the fault the main puzzle |
| The grid is hard to read | Prototype the grid map and the line visuals first; playtest early |
| The story's reveals arrive too fast or too slow | Use context sources, especially machines read through the serpent, to pace reveals; let curious players find more, but never require it |
| Ending version one at the core gives away too much of the full game | Keep the reveal to "Light is a machine"; hold back humanity's fate and the Cherub |
| Version one feels like a demo rather than a complete experience | Give it a full arc: a problem, a journey, a climax, and a changed hero |
