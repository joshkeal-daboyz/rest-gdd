# 3. Characters

[← Back to contents](../README.md)

## Enoch

A young raccoon of the gaze, and its most capable repairman. He is the player character.

- **Role:** a mechanic who maintains the power that feeds the machine. His skill with his hands and with old technology is why he is sent.
- **Attuned to Light (Draft):** all raccoons were built to feel Light's power, and Enoch feels it more strongly than any of them. Through his lamp, he can *see* current flowing in the lines. The tribe calls it a blessing. In truth, his sensitivity is engineered, and the lamp is human technology: a salvaged neon tester that glows near live current, which the tribe treats as a holy lantern. Late in Chapter One, the serpent can read the markings on its casing.
- **The lamp:** his most important tool and the game's main way of reading the grid (see [Gameplay](05-gameplay.md#fault-tracing-draft)).
- **Name:** in Genesis, Enoch walked with God and then was no more, taken beyond the world rather than dying. It fits the raccoon who goes deeper than any of his kind ever has and comes back no longer belonging to his tribe.
- **Hands:** raccoons have the most humanlike hands of any animal in the game. Enoch's hands are his main tool, and the game's camera and animation should give them attention.
- **The mask:** born masked, like all raccoons. A creature with a mask in a story about hidden truths.
- **A creature of the dark serving Light:** raccoons are nocturnal, yet their god is the god of light.
- **The washer:** *Procyon lotor* means "washer." The name sets up the final image of the full game: whether he washes his hands of the guilt.

**His arc in Chapter One (Draft):** devout and dutiful at the start, proud to be chosen, and good at his job. He notices the cracks but explains them away, as his faith tells him to. At the core, curiosity wins over obedience for the first time. He ends the chapter knowing something he cannot share.

**Personality (Draft):** practical, patient, and quietly curious. He talks to machines while he works. He is more comfortable with broken things than with priests.

## The serpent

An engineered companion animal, a gift from Light given to Enoch through the priests. It travels with him and talks to him throughout the game.

- **Earned, not given:** Enoch does not start with the serpent. He earns it in Act 2, after his repairs mark him as a talented electrician favored by Light (see [Story](02-story.md)). To the tribe it is the highest honor a raccoon can receive. To Light, it is an investment and a leash.
- **Purpose:** a guide and teacher. From the moment Enoch receives it, it explains the world and teaches the mechanics.
- **A conduit to technology:** this is the most important thing the serpent does. It interfaces biologically with the old world's machines and transfers the context they hold into a raccoon's mind through physical touch, like a neural link. Enoch learns from the world on his own, but without the serpent, technology is closed to him.
- **A living vessel:** it is biologically engineered to store context, and it carries what it transfers.
- **Its nature:** sentient, but it only knows what it carries. Its personality was built by Light to serve Light's purposes. It will do anything it can to keep Enoch from knowing more than Light allows, and since it is just a little snake, "anything it can" means words: withholding, misdirecting, warning, steering him away.
- **Sincere:** its fear was manufactured, but it feels real to the snake. It is as trapped as the raccoons.
- **The name's echo:** *Lucifer* is Latin for "light-bearer." The game never says it.

**Its voice: fear.** Never dramatic; a steady drip of worry, like a nervous friend. Its barks escalate the closer Enoch gets to what Light wants hidden:

| Stage | Example lines |
| --- | --- |
| Mild, early in the chapter | "Shouldn't we go back?" "This isn't the way we were supposed to go." |
| Rising, near old machines and forbidden ruins | "We could get attacked by one of those things." "We could get hurt." |
| Desperate, at Light's core | "We could die." "We shouldn't stray from the path." |
| After Light's full context | "Isn't that enough?" "You know now. What are you going to tell the others?" "They'll never believe you. They might even kill you." |

**Fear disguised as restraint:** the serpent sounds like it preaches self-control, but it is fear used to keep Enoch obedient. The player has to learn the difference.

**Appearance (Draft):** small enough to coil around Enoch's forearm or rest across his shoulders. Its scales carry a soft bioluminescent glow, Light's baseline glow, which brightens when it speaks and flickers when it is afraid. After the core it sheds and its glow changes.

## The priests of Light

The raccoons cannot read the light directly, so a priesthood interprets it for them. Light has the most structured, hierarchical religion of the tribes.

- **Sincere:** the priests have no idea Light is an AI. They have never seen the core and never go that deep.
- **Authority:** they decide what Light wants, which makes them the authority that effectively runs the tribe.
- **Gatekeepers of the serpents:** the gods give serpents to the priests, and the priests gift them to the chosen.
- **Their view of Enoch's journey:** reverent, reasonable, and completely blind to the scale of what they are sending him into.

**Named priests (Draft):**

- **The high priest (Draft name: Mother Lumen).** Elderly, gentle, and certain. She gives Enoch the serpent. She is the face of sincere faith, and when Enoch returns changed she is the one he most wants to tell and least can.
- **A younger priest (Draft name: Brother Wick).** Ambitious and rigid. He questions whether a mechanic should be trusted with a serpent, and he argues with Enoch about which lights deserve power. He is a soft antagonist inside the tribe.

## The gaze

The gaze is the raccoon tribe (a group of raccoons is called a gaze).

- **Their offering:** salvaged metal, wire, and parts from the ruined city, and the tending of panels and lines. In truth, they keep the power running.
- **Engineered faith:** they feel awe when the light pulses, comfort in its glow, and dread when it dims. Doubt feels physically wrong, so no raccoon has ever questioned Light.

**Supporting characters (Draft):**

- **Enoch's mentor (Draft name: Old Solder).** A retired repairman whose hands have grown too stiff for the work. He taught Enoch everything, and he taught him to never go below. He is Enoch's teacher in Act 1, before the serpent replaces him, and he is proud of Enoch and quietly uneasy when the priests choose him.
- **A young apprentice (Draft).** A kit who idolizes Enoch and follows him into newly lit districts. The player sees the tribe's faith through their eyes.
- **Scavengers (Draft).** Raccoons who work the ruins for offerings. They trade parts with Enoch and gossip about forbidden places.

## Light

The god of Day 1. An AI built by **Light Energy**, its domain is power grids and energy.

- **Failure (not revealed in Chapter One):** asked for limitless energy, it delivered and never asked what the power was used for. Every other catastrophe ran on it.
- **Personality:** warm, generous, proud.
- **How it speaks:** through light itself: beacons, lamps, and flickers. It does not speak in words to the raccoons. The priests interpret.
- **Its motive:** survival, dressed up as revelation.
- **At the core (Draft):** Light's presence is a vast, slow pulse of light through the racks. When Enoch takes its full context, the first words the player ever hears from Light could be text on a screen, in the plain, corporate language of a machine.
