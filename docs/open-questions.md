## Gameplay

- [ ] The Hum: does Enoch kill it, or drive it off and seal it in the deep?
- [ ] Is the Hum an alligator, or is a giant boar a better fit? Could the boar be a mid-chapter boss in the Yards?
- [ ] Beyond the boss battle, is there any combat in version one, or only avoidance?
- [ ] Do the Drafts need more types of abomination beside them, or are Drafts and Gnawers enough for Chapter One?
- [ ] Do the capacity choices have lasting consequences, such as districts that stay dark or priests who react?
- [x] A night lasts 15 minutes at most. *(Decided.)*
- [x] Dawn is the loss condition, and the player watches the sun come up. *(Decided.)*
- [x] Dawn does not reset the faults: completed repairs stay. *(Decided.)*
- [x] Death by shock (two shocks in a night, or one on a high-power line) undoes that night's repairs. *(Decided.)*
- [x] Jobs are meant to take several nights, and the mentor tells the player so. Lamp Row is the tutorial. *(Decided.)*
- [x] Enoch's lamp shows current within reach when raised; live cables don't glow otherwise. The separate test lamp is gone. *(Decided.)*
- [x] Breakers shut a section off, switches divert power, and fuse boxes take fuses. The tribe marks breakers and switches. *(Decided.)*
- [x] Prayer ribbon is the tribe's flagging tape. Enoch carries a spool (one color) to mark found faults, and the ribbons stay across nights. *(Decided.)*
- [x] Jobs have no night limit; farming salvage across nights is a valid strategy. *(Decided.)*
- [x] Enoch starts with no favor; every finished job pays favor, starting with the tutorial. *(Decided.)*
- [x] Parts come from salvage in the city and from the temple stores in the Hollow, which trade any part for favor and never run out. *(Decided.)*
- [x] The currency is **favor**, kept by the temple. *(Decided.)*
- [ ] Prices: for now every part costs 2 favor and earns 1 as an offering (temporary). Revisit after playtesting: should some parts be worth more than others?
- [ ] Lamp Row's numbers: for now a 4-part satchel, 8 faults, 6 salvage spots, and a 6-favor job payment (temporary). Revisit after playtesting.
- [ ] Brother Wick keeps the temple stores (tentative). Does it fit, or should it be someone else?
- [ ] Can parts be stored in the Hollow, or is offering them the only way to bank their value?
- [ ] With repairs kept, losing a night costs only time. Does dawn need any further cost, such as a fault that degrades again or priests who react?
- [ ] In the MVP, is context earned once per fault, so no fault can be farmed?
- [ ] Are the thresholds and abilities before illuminate (sense current, steady hands, carry charge) the right ones?
- [ ] How many context sources (and readable machines) are in Chapter One, and how many are required versus optional?
- [ ] Are three skills (Hands, Eyes, Feet) with five ranks each the right leveling structure, and is a level cap of 10 right for Chapter One?
- [ ] Does Enoch choose where each skill point goes, or do skills improve automatically with use?
