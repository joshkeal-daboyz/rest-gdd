# 8. Art and Audio

[← Back to contents](../README.md)

All entries in this section are Draft.

## Visual direction

- **A lit ruin.** A human city reclaimed by plants, water, and time, with pools of warm light surviving in the dark. The image the game is built around: a single working streetlight in an overgrown street.
- **Night first.** Most of the game takes place at night. The palette is deep blues and greens in the dark, with warm amber, white, and gold where the power runs.
- **Light as progress.** Every repaired district changes the city's silhouette. From the Spires, the player should be able to look out and see what they have lit.
- **The tribe's craft over human ruin.** Raccoon architecture is patched together from salvage: wire-wrapped lamps, shrines made of fuse boxes, prayer flags of old cable. The tribe paints and ties prayer ribbon on every breaker, switch, and fuse box, so the grid's controls stand out from the ruin. Human structures are massive, square, and indifferent.
- **Corporate traces.** The *Light* logo is clean, modern, and out of place, and it repeats everywhere. It should read as an ordinary brand to the player and as holy script in the raccoons' craft.
- **The depths turn cold.** The Underworks and the core shift from warm amber to clean, cold white and blue: the color of a server room. The temple's warmth ends where the machine begins.

## Characters

- **Raccoons:** upright and humanoid, with usable hands and speech, while keeping a raccoon's build, mask, and tail. Clothing is practical and layered, made of salvage.
- **Enoch:** a tool belt, a satchel, a work apron, a spool of prayer ribbon, and his lamp (a salvaged neon tester the tribe treats as a holy lantern). He is designed by the creative director in hand-drawn concept art, drawn in the city pack's style, and generated in Tripo from those drawings. His working animation sells the repairs.
- **The serpent:** small and expressive, with a soft glow along its scales that acts as its face.
- **The priests:** robes in light and gold, with lamps worn as vestments.
- **Abominations:** wrong, but not gory. They are failed genetic experiments, so they should look like unfinished animals: proportions that don't quite work, features that didn't form, and traces of the tribes they were meant to become. The Drafts should be just recognizable as raccoons.
- **The Hum:** an enormous, ancient alligator, scarred and overgrown, closer to a landscape than an animal when it is still. Its scale should read from the first moment it moves.

## Lighting as a system

Light carries gameplay information, so it must be consistent:

| Light | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Warm, steady amber | Safe, restored, the tribe's light |
| Flickering | Failing, under load, or with lines chewed by Gnawers |
| Pulses in a cable (only inside Enoch's lamp light) | Live current, flowing away from the source |
| Sparks | A damaged cable on a live line |
| Prayer ribbon | A fault Enoch has found and marked |
| Cold white or blue | The machine: the depths, the core, the truth |
| The serpent's glow | Its mood: bright when calm, flickering when afraid |
| Darkness | Unknown and unrestored |

### Cables in the dark

- **Live cables don't glow.** In the dark, every cable looks the same, which keeps the atmosphere. Current is only visible as pulses inside the light of Enoch's raised lamp. The Relight is the first time the player sees a whole street's power at once.
- **Every cable is a grid cable.** Decorative wires are thin, rusted, and end in frayed stubs, so they never read as part of the grid.
- **Prayer ribbon** is the tribe's flagging tape, and in truth it *is* old human flagging tape, salvaged by the spool. It is the one saturated color in a dark street, so marked faults and holy lines are visible from far away. The same ribbon marks the tribe's holy lines and Enoch's faults.

## Audio direction

- **The hum.** The grid has a voice. Live lines hum, louder as Enoch gets closer, and damaged lines crackle from their position in the street, so a player can find faults by ear. The hum is part of the music. A district coming back online should feel like a chord resolving.
- **The quiet.** Dead districts are near-silent: wind, water, and distant creatures.
- **Hands.** Close, tactile foley for every repair: wire twisting, fuses seating, breakers clunking, switches clacking. A shock is loud, sudden, and physical.
- **Music (Draft).** Sparse and warm in the Hollow, building with each restored district, and turning cold and electronic in the depths. At the core, the music should drop out.
- **Voices.** Every character speaks English, for the sake of storytelling. *(Decided.)* The serpent is fully voiced, quietly and intimately.
- **Light's voice.** Light never speaks in words to the raccoons. At the core, it speaks to Enoch directly *(Decided)*, through text, tone, and pulses of light (Draft).
