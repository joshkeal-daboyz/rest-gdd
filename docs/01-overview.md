## High concept

A young raccoon mechanic repairs the dying lights of a dead human city for a god he has never seen, and the deeper he goes into the grid, the more he learns that his god is a machine.

## Elevator pitch

Enoch is a repairman of the gaze, the raccoon tribe that serves Light. The city's lights are dimming, and the priests send the tribe's mechanics out to mend them. Enoch's work sets him apart, and he earns the tribe's highest honor: a talking serpent from the god, and the duty to descend into the city where no other mechanic is sent. With the serpent as his guide, he traces dead lines, salvages parts from human ruins, and brings district after district back to light. Every repair takes him closer to the core beneath the city, and to the forbidden truth of what Light really is.

## Design pillars

1. **Hands, not weapons.** Enoch is a mechanic, not a warrior. Problems are solved by understanding and fixing things: tracing faults, salvaging parts, rerouting power. Danger is avoided, outwitted, or survived, rarely fought.
2. **Light is language.** Light is how the god speaks, how the tribe feels, how the player reads the world, and how progress is shown. A restored district is a visible, lit change to the city.
3. **The cracks show.** The world is built so that the player notices what Enoch's faith explains away: logos on temple walls, warehouses used as shrines, machinery behind miracles. The truth is found, not told.
4. **Restraint is the virtue.** The power source is failing, and there is never enough to light everything. Choosing what not to take is as important as taking. This sets up the game's central theme: lack of self-control is the original sin.
5. **Fear as a compass.** The serpent's worry rises the closer Enoch gets to what Light wants hidden. The guide meant to keep him on the path is what leads him off it.

## Themes in version one

The full game carries all of the Lore Bible's themes. Chapter One introduces three of them:

- **Engineered faith.** The raccoons feel awe, comfort, and dread in response to the light because Light built them that way.
- **Rationed truth.** The priests ration Light's truth to the tribe, and the serpent rations it to Enoch.
- **The first temptation.** At the core, Enoch takes more context than the repair requires. This is the game's first Eve moment.

## Audience

- Players of story-driven exploration games who enjoy discovering a world's secrets through its environment (Draft).
- Comparable tone and structure: a quiet, melancholy world revealed through exploration, with a companion guide in the role of Destiny's Ghost or Ocarina of Time's Navi.

## What makes it different

- **Repair as the main verb.** Progress comes from making a broken system work again, not from defeating enemies.
- **A companion who is working against you.** The serpent is sincere, kind, and afraid, and its whole purpose is to keep you from knowing too much.
- **A world made from a specification.** Genesis is the AIs' template for creation, so every system in the game rhymes with scripture, and every piece of scripture turns out to be infrastructure or marketing.
