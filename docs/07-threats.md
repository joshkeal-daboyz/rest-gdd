# 7. Threats and Hazards

[← Back to contents](../README.md)

Enoch is a mechanic, not a warrior. Danger in Chapter One is something to avoid, outwit, or survive. Combat is out of scope for version one (Draft).

## Abominations of Light

The AIs' failed experiments survive as abominations: less intelligent, dangerous creatures found deep in the facilities and out in the wastes. Light's abominations are **creatures warped by power and drawn to it, clustering around live lines and generators**.

This gives the repair loop a built-in tension: **every repair makes the city more dangerous.** Restoring power draws abominations to the lines Enoch just brought back.

### Abomination types (Draft)

| Name (Draft) | Behavior | Counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Moths** | Small, swarming, harmless alone. Cluster around lit endpoints and short them out. | Cut power to scatter them, or draw them off with a decoy light |
| **Drinkers** | Large, slow creatures that latch onto live lines and drain them, causing brownouts. | Lure them to a dead line, or overload the line to drive them off |
| **Arcers** | Fast predators that move along live cables and strike from them. | Stay off powered ground, cut sections of the grid, use darkness |
| **The Hum (Draft boss)** | A huge, half-buried thing in the Underworks, grown around a main line. | A sequence of switching and rerouting to starve it of power |

### The light and the dark

- In dead areas, Enoch is safe from abominations of Light but must deal with darkness: limited visibility, hidden drops, and getting lost.
- In live areas, he can see but is exposed to the creatures drawn to power.
- Switching power on and off becomes a tool: cut a line to send creatures away, restore it once he's past.

## Environmental hazards

| Hazard | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Live lines | Touching a live line shocks Enoch and knocks him back. Steady hands (threshold 1) removes this for repairs. |
| Surges | Switching in the wrong order sends a surge down the line, blowing fuses and endpoints. |
| Falls | The city is vertical, and the Spires especially so. |
| Collapse | Ruined floors and passages give way. |
| Water and power | Flooded basements near live lines are lethal until the power is cut. |
| Daylight (Draft) | Soft pressure to return to the Hollow before dawn. |

## Failure (Draft)

- Being caught or badly shocked sends Enoch back to the last lit safe point, such as a shrine or repaired junction. Repairs already completed stay completed.
- There is no permanent loss and no game over screen. The world's tone is melancholy, not punishing.

## Foreshadowing the full game

- **Smoke on the horizon.** Fire is forbidden to every tribe but the dogs. From the Spires, Enoch sees smoke at the border, and the serpent avoids the subject.
- **Scorched ground.** At the very edge of the city, a burned ruin marks a place where something was destroyed. The serpent calls it a warning.
- **An owl (Draft).** Once, an owl watches Enoch from a high perch and is gone when he looks again.
