# 6. Progression

[← Back to contents](../README.md)

Progress in Chapter One happens on two tracks:

- **The grid:** restoring districts opens the city and moves the story forward.
- **The serpent:** collecting context fragments strengthens the serpent, which grants Enoch new abilities.

## Context fragments

Collecting fragments of context is how the player levels up in each chapter. Fragments feed the serpent, and once it holds enough information, the player can move forward.

- **Where they're found:** ruins, old machines, records, and places the priests' taboos keep animals away from. Many are artifacts of the old world: logos, signage, and other corporate traces.
- **Fragments versus scripture:** what the player finds in the ruins doesn't always match what the priests teach. Leveling up and discovering the deception are the same activity.
- **How Enoch takes them (Draft):** he touches an old machine or record with the serpent, and the serpent absorbs it. Some fragments need power to read, which ties them back to the grid.
- **What the player sees (Draft):** a short image, sound, or text the serpent passes to Enoch, plus an entry in the journal written in Enoch's words, as he understands it. His interpretation is filtered through faith early and becomes plainer later.

### Fragment types (Draft)

| Type | Example | What it teaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signage | A *Light* logo on a utility van | The god's name is written everywhere by the ancients |
| Records | A maintenance schedule | The holy rites look like a work rota |
| Machines | A terminal that still boots | The ancients built things that think |
| Places | A sealed control room | The sacred depths were a workplace |
| Echoes | A recorded announcement | The ancients spoke to Light, and Light obeyed |

## Thresholds and shedding

At each threshold, the serpent sheds its skin and emerges changed, and its touch grants Enoch a new ability. Shed skins can be left at the temple as relics (Draft).

The full game has one major ability per god. For Chapter One, the draft below splits Light's progression into smaller thresholds that build toward illuminate at the core.

| Threshold | When | Ability (Draft) | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Receiving the serpent | **Sense current** | Hold the serpent near a line to read whether it is live, dead, or damaged |
| 1 | After Lamp Row | **Steady hands** | Live-line repairs no longer shock Enoch, which opens repairs that previously required cutting power |
| 2 | After the Yards | **Carry charge** | Enoch holds a short charge in his hands to jump a gap in a circuit or briefly power a door or lift |
| 3 | At the core, after taking Light's full context | **Illuminate** | Reveal hidden paths, buried machinery, and things the gods don't want seen |

### Illuminate

Light's ability in the full game. Enoch earns it at the end of Chapter One.

- Enoch releases a pulse of light that reveals what is hidden: buried cables, sealed doors, faded writing, and the machinery behind miracles.
- After the core, the player can return through the city and see it differently. Logos become readable, shrines reveal their wiring, and the temple's altar shows its company name.
- In the full game, illuminate opens paths in other realms, so visit order changes which areas open first without branching the story.

## The serpent changes

After each threshold the serpent changes a little: a new turn of phrase, a new color in its light, a new piece of its form. The player's progress is visible in their companion.

- In Chapter One, its glow grows brighter and steadier with each shed.
- At the core, after the overload, its glow changes to something new. This is Light's serpent change: its baseline glow, since the serpent is Light's own creation.

## Repair skill (Draft)

Enoch begins as an expert, so the game should not make him clumsy. His toolkit expands instead:

- New tools found or traded (a better splicer, a fuse puller, insulated gloves).
- New repair types introduced by each district.
- Satchel upgrades for carrying more parts.
