# 4. World: The City of Light

[← Back to contents](../README.md)

## The realm

Light's realm is a **ruined city kept lit long after its people died**. It sits in a compact region built around a former data center cluster, where rival corporations built side by side for shared power, cooling water, and fiber. Light's seat is the Light Energy facility beneath the city.

Light powers the other gods, so its city is the hub of the region's grid. Main lines run out of the city toward the other realms, and in Chapter One those lines are the visible threads of what lies beyond (Draft).

## How the city tells the story

- **Faith built on infrastructure.** Shrines are built around substations, altars around fuse boxes, and holy lamps on old streetlight poles.
- **Brand names as scripture.** The word *Light* appears everywhere on old signage: on poles, panels, vans, and buildings. To the raccoons it is the god's name written by the ancients. The player will recognize it as a logo.
- **Lit and unlit.** Where the power runs, the raccoons live. Where it doesn't, the city belongs to the dark and the things in it. Restoring power literally expands the tribe's world.
- **Taboos as walls.** The priests forbid the depths, the sealed doors, and certain marked buildings. Every taboo protects machinery Light doesn't want seen.

## Districts (Draft)

The city is divided into districts, each tied to a section of the grid. Each one introduces a new repair system and a new crack in the faith.

### The Hollow — the gaze's home

- **What it is:** the raccoon settlement, built in a sunken plaza and the basements around it, beneath the Temple of Light.
- **Grid role:** always powered, the last thing Light lets go dark. It begins to flicker in the prologue.
- **Gameplay role:** hub and tutorial. Mentor, traders, and the priests are here. The vendor is here too: Enoch sells salvage and buys the parts each job needs before he heads out.
- **Crack in the faith:** the temple's great lamp is an old stadium floodlight.

### The Temple of Light

- **What it is:** the priests' seat. Built into the lobby of a former Light Energy office building.
- **Gameplay role:** story hub. The priests react to each restored district here.
- **Crack in the faith:** a company logo is carved behind the altar, worn smooth by generations of hands. With illuminate, it becomes readable.

### Lamp Row

- **What it is:** a long commercial street of collapsed storefronts and streetlights.
- **Grid role:** a simple lighting circuit fed from the Hollow.
- **Gameplay role:** the tutorial job and the first open repair area. It teaches reading lines with the lamp, marking faults, cutting the power at a breaker, salvage, planning what to carry, and simple repairs. It is short: the head of the street to its far end and back takes about a minute.
- **Crack in the faith:** shop signs and advertisements of people using light for ordinary things.

### The Yards

- **What it is:** a substation and transformer yard surrounded by fences, warning signs, and a field of old solar panels.
- **Grid role:** feeds most of the city. Restoring it makes several districts possible.
- **Gameplay role:** the first large repair. It teaches load, capacity, and switching. The Yards have been dark for generations, so this is where Enoch first meets the abominations.
- **Crack in the faith:** maintenance logs and schedules that read like a liturgy.

### The Spires

- **What it is:** a cluster of high-rises, with a beacon at the top of the tallest.
- **Grid role:** the beacon is how Light speaks to the whole city.
- **Gameplay role:** vertical traversal, climbing, and a beacon-restart sequence. The highest vantage point in the realm.
- **Crack in the faith:** from the top, Enoch sees the edges of Light's lands, the other realms, and smoke rising at the border.

### The Underworks

- **What it is:** cable tunnels, maintenance shafts, and service corridors beneath the city, leading down to the facility.
- **Grid role:** the main lines from the core.
- **Gameplay role:** the descent. Darker, tighter, more dangerous, and with heavier taboo markings.
- **Crack in the faith:** the sacred depths look less like a holy place and more like a building with every step.

### The Core

- **What it is:** Light's seat, the machine core of the Light Energy data center.
- **Gameplay role:** the final repair, the diagnosis, and the first temptation.
- **Crack in the faith:** there is nothing left to explain away.

## Day and night (Draft)

- Raccoons are nocturnal, so the tribe's day is the night. The game mostly takes place after dark, which is when the lights matter.
- Daylight drives the raccoons underground, so each night is a race against dawn. Losing a night costs time, not work: repairs hold until Enoch returns. This is the game's loss condition (see [Threats and Hazards](07-threats.md#win-and-loss-draft)).

## Beyond Light's lands (Version one)

The edges of the realm are visible but not reachable in version one: the cold peaks of Signs, the storm highlands of Firmament, overgrown farmland, a flooded coast, a jungle, and at the center, Eden, ringed by ash. Main power lines running toward each realm hint at Light's dependence on the others.
