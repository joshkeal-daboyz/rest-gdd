# Rest — Game Design Document

**Rest** is a narrative exploration game set on Earth several hundred years after humanity's collapse. Masterless AIs have engineered intelligent animals to serve as the hands that will bring humanity back, and the animals worship those AIs as gods, never knowing they are tools.

You play **Enoch**, a young raccoon and repairman in service to Light, the god of power. The first version of the game is **Chapter One: Light**: an introduction to Light's ruined city, where Enoch works to repair the failing power grid and bring the city's lights back.

> This repository holds the game design document (GDD). The world, history, and themes are defined in the [Lore Bible](Rest%20%E2%80%94%20Lore%20Bible.md), which is the source of truth for story. The GDD describes how that story becomes a game.

## Status conventions

- **Decided:** settled in the Lore Bible or confirmed by the design lead.
- **Draft:** a proposal written for this GDD. It is open to change and needs sign-off.

Unmarked statements that restate the Lore Bible are decided.
